OUR STORY

We've Felt The Fear On The Crease.

Between the pipes, there's a split-second flinch before every shot — the fear of getting hit and going down mid-practice. That fear doesn't just sting. It holds you back. It keeps goalies from training at full speed, from playing to their potential, from trusting their gear when it matters most.

No lacrosse goalie should have to practice afraid of getting seriously hurt. That's the problem we're building ROVO to solve — equipment that lets you stop flinching and start playing.
