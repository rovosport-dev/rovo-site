// PROBLEM 02

Hand injuries.

The second problem we are solving first, and one I have lived through twice.

I have broken my hand twice as a lacrosse goalie. It was expensive, and it cost me months of training.

In my experience, there is no glove out there that is currently good enough. There are so many on the market, but they are not protective enough and they are not helping us enough.

Many of these injuries come out of practice. If you take one off the hand, there are several different places that can get hurt, and those are the places we aim to solve. Bruises on the hands and thumbs are common too.

Sam Cooper · Co-founder, former Dartmouth D1 goalie
