// 01 HOW IT STARTED

Two friends. One problem.

We met on the same freshman-year floor at Dartmouth. Sam came home from lacrosse practice every day with new bruises and injuries, and Richard saw them all. We became close friends and knew we wanted to build something together.

Sam started working on solutions in the summer of 2022 and has been at it ever since. Now, with the time to go full force, we are building better protection for the athletes who need it.

FROM SAM

I couldn't stop thinking about it. There are just so many clear problems for goalies. I graduated from Dartmouth in June 2026 and quit my job to chase my dream of building a brand around solving a problem that helps people. I love building products. My passion is building, engineering, and using creativity to bring ideas to life.
